Air-quality stations in Sabah

Sabah has six JAS (Department of Environment) stations: Politeknik KK, Kota Kinabalu, Kimanis, Keningau, Sandakan and Tawau. Labuan is a separate federal territory. The CPRC reports use Politeknik KK, the nearest station to Tuaran. This chart is a single snapshot at the haze peak (5 pm, 2 Sep), as released by JAS Sabah. All six stations were Unhealthy.

Source: JAS Sabah via Borneo Post, 2 Sep 2026. For daily readings from every station, export hourly IPU from APIMS (MyJAS EQMS) and add it to the dashboard.

Interpretation and data notes

  • Start: the IPU crossed 100 on 31 Aug (122) and peaked at 178 on 2 Sep. The SPOTREP records more than 150 for 24 hours (171–185), so the operations room opened at 8.00 am on 3 Sep, in line with the KKM Haze Consequence Management Action Plan (2020).
  • End: the IPU has been Moderate since 8 Sep (93). Haze surveillance last appears in the 13 Sep report, and from 14 Sep the operations room status isn't stated. No closing SPOTREP was found. Confirm the official deactivation date.
  • Rainfall: Tuaran recorded essentially no rain from early August to mid-September (only 1.5 mm on 31 Aug). The first meaningful rain was 16 mm on 16 Sep, and the IPU fell to 38 (Good) on 17 Sep. MET thunderstorm forecasts became frequent from 10 Sep. This fits the haze being sustained by the dry southwest-monsoon spell and washed out once rain returned.
  • Second spike: the IPU was 151 on both 20 and 21 Sep, with no reactivation. These are snapshot readings, so check JAS hourly data to see whether the 24-hour trigger was met.
  • Case peak lag: cases peaked on 9–12 Sep, after the IPU had dropped. This matches the addition of private clinics from 9 Sep (1–4 facilities rising to 8–18), so it's partly a reporting effect.
  • Check: the 9 Sep URTI counts for four government clinics (7, 3, 9, 19) exactly match the 8 Sep asthma counts, and the 38 asthma cases on 8 Sep are unusually high. Verify both against the PDF.
  • Respiratory: influenza A notifications rose sharply from late September and peaked at 30–39 a day in late October to early November. That was well after the haze cleared, so it is a separate seasonal influenza signal, not a haze effect. The 5 avian influenza notifications (2 Oct – 23 Oct) should be checked for their verification status.
  • Sabah stations: the station map uses 16 snapshot readings published by JAS Sabah, APIMS and JPBN through the news (sources listed under each snapshot). They are not complete daily series. For full hourly data, export it from APIMS / MyJAS EQMS.
  • No CPRC reports for 23 Aug, 26 Aug, 5–6 Sep, 19 Sep, 24–25 Oct or 5 Nov (shown as gaps). The station is in Kota Kinabalu, not inside Tuaran district.
Source: Laporan Harian Bilik Gerakan CPRC PKK Tuaran (91 reports, 1 Aug – 7 Nov 2026) and SPOTREP Pembukaan Bilik Gerakan Bencana Jerebu, 3 Sep 2026. Prepared for the DrPH Field Posting VI (Environmental Health).
